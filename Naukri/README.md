## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes a **Naukri.com job postings dataset** using SQL to uncover insights about job roles, companies, required skills, experience levels, and salary trends.

The goal of this project is to help job seekers and analysts understand:
- Which skills are most in demand  
- Which industries hire the most  
- What experience levels companies prefer  
- Average salaries across roles and locations  
- How job descriptions vary across sectors  

This project demonstrates practical SQL for **data cleaning, transformation, and exploratory data analysis (EDA)**.

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Answered

### **1️⃣ Job Market Insights**
- Which job roles are most frequently posted?
- What are the top hiring companies?
- Which cities have the highest job openings?

### **2️⃣ Salary Trends**
- What is the average salary by job role?
- Which locations offer the highest salary?
- How does salary vary by experience?

### **3️⃣ Skill Demand Analysis**
- What are the most in-demand skills across job roles?
- Which tech skills dominate the market?
- Skill frequency comparisons between roles (e.g., Data Analyst vs ML Engineer)

### **4️⃣ Experience-Level Insights**
- What experience range is most commonly required?
- Entry-level vs mid-level vs senior job distribution

### **5️⃣ Industry Insights**
- Which industries hire the most?
- Industry-wise salary breakdown
- Trends by company sector (IT, Finance, Healthcare, etc.)

## ✨ Insights Summary

📌 **Data Analyst**, **Software Engineer**, and **Machine Learning roles** appear most frequently  
📌 Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Pune lead in job postings  
📌 Salary strongly correlates with experience, especially for technical roles  
📌 Top skills include: Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Machine Learning  
📌 IT and Consulting are the most active hiring industries  
📌 Companies increasingly prioritize multi-skill candidates
